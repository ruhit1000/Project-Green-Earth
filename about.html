About Green Earth

We're on a mission to make the planet greener,
one tree at a time. Join us in creating a sustainable future.

Our Mission

Green Earth was founded with a simple but powerful vision: to combat climate change through large-scale tree plantation. We believe that by empowering communities and individuals to plant trees, we can create lasting environmental change.

Since 2020, we've been working tirelessly to restore degraded forests, create green spaces in urban areas, and educate people about the importance of trees in our ecosystem. Every tree planted is a step towards a healthier planet.

Our Journey

2020

The Beginning

Green Earth was founded with a mission to plant 1 million trees. Started with just 5 volunteers and planted our first 1,000 trees.

2022

Global Expansion

Expanded operations to 50+ countries and reached 100,000 trees planted. Partnered with local communities worldwide.

2024

Half Million Milestone

Celebrated planting our 500,000th tree. Launched digital platform for tracking individual contributions and environmental impact.

2026

Present Day

Continuing our mission with renewed energy. On track to reach 1 million trees by 2027. Join us in making history!
